MSc Dissertation — ANSYS Fluent, URANS

Hypersonic Inlet Buzz at Mach 5

2D URANS simulations of hypersonic inlet buzz using a k-ω SST model and third-order MUSCL discretisation on a 296k-element structured grid. Open and blocked isolator configurations validated qualitatively against schlieren data.

1548 Hz / 1444 Hz buzz freq within 6.5% of theory

MSc Group Project — ANSYS System Coupling

Fluid–Structure Interaction of Civil Aircraft During Rapid Decompression

Two-fidelity FSI methodology simulating explosive decompression of an Airbus A320 fuselage, coupling CFD and FEA solvers. Modelled structural response and cabin acoustics across multiple panel layouts and breach sizes, with a grid convergence study validating the framework.

15 Hz buffeting 131–149 dB SPL range
